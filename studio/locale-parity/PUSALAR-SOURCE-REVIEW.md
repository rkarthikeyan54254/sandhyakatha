## Evidence relevant to repair

4175–4178 describe unsuccessful fundraising, construction in the mind over a
long period, and preparation for consecration. 4179–4180 give Śiva's dream
instruction to postpone the king's ceremony. They do not say Pūsalār fixed his
date first. 4181–4186 describe the king's wish to honor the devotee, his personal
visit rather than summoning him, and reverence on learning of the mental temple.
4187 continues with consecration and sustained worship.

## Current canonical/locale discrepancies

- English's competition framing (the temple that "had beaten his") changes the
  king's motive. The primary witness supplies reverence, not that motive.

  other scene particulars need separate support or removal. A broad ledger

- The current locale follow-up 2 replaces the English question about the king's
  temple with a question about postponement. Equal counts did not ensure equal
  parent material; the coordinated review must resolve this explicitly.
