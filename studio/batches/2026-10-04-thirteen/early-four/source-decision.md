- None of these four has a defensible 3-minute and 6-minute English pair yet. Producing 650–680 spoken words from the currently inspected snippets would require invented scenes, violating `EDITORIAL.md`.
- No Hindi/Tamil edition, image, media row, public selector, lock, or publication state should be created from this checkpoint. The native-language moat and human `languageEditor` approval remain separate hard gates after a canonical English source ledger and narration are stable.
- Prioritize locating exact witnesses for `lakshmana-rekha` and `narsinh-hundi`, and an explicit editorial decision for `kabir-shroud`. For `mira-cup`, correct the hook and decide whether the selected poem supports a full-length narrative without later inventions.
