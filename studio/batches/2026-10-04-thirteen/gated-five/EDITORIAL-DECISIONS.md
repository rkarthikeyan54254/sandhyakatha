## Required before any promotion

1. Reconcile the canon hook/care-note discrepancies noted above; retain the five age gates and accurate sensitivity flags. In particular, add explicit self-injury/suicide care treatment where the schema permits, and do not imply that miraculous restoration cancels ordinary harm.
2. Build a claim-by-claim source ledger from the specified passages, distinguishing Sēkkiḻār's Tamil hagiography from earlier hymn witnesses and distinguishing the selected *Śiva Purāṇa* telling from other Dakṣa versions.
3. Author independent 3- and 6-minute English tellings with parent-only asides and full `ifTheyAsk` answers. Avoid turning source or care notes into spoken narration. Run the repository's strict content and timing checks.
4. Author Hindi and Tamil from the canonical claims and narrative sequence, not English sentence shape. Run the native-language gates; leave each `languageEditor` pending until a human native-language reviewer has read the rendered story aloud and approved it.
5. Review rendered cards, age-gate disclosure, and all three reader surfaces with a parent before publication. No story from this group should be promoted merely because automated validation passes.
