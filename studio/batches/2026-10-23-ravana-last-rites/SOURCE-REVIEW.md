was inspected. It supports Vibhīṣaṇa’s grief, fallen crown and arms, rejected
counsel, tree/elephant/fire comparisons, Rāma’s response about courage and duty,
the recollection of generosity and sacred practices, the request and initial
funeral instruction. The ethical stance is not an endorsement of Rāvaṇa’s acts.

[Received-text Yuddha 111](https://www.valmikiramayan.net/utf8/yuddha/sarga111/yuddha_111_prose.htm)
was inspected. It supports Mandodarī’s lament, rejected counsel and Sītā’s
abduction; Vibhīṣaṇa’s later objection; repeated funeral instruction; priests,
wood, fires, bier and procession; offerings, consolation and return; and Rāma
laying aside arms, armour and anger. It also reports happiness after victory.
Thus the submitted claim that he did not celebrate is removed. Funeral details
are selected without graphic description. The chapter contains additional ritual
details not recited here; no claim of exhaustive translation is made.
