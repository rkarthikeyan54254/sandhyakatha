# Gārgī — source ledger

Checked 2026-10-08. Witness: F. Max Müller, *The Upanishads*, Part II, Sacred Books of the East 15 (Oxford, 1884), Bṛhadāraṇyaka III.8.1–12. [Complete public-domain transcription, Eighth Brāhmaṇa](https://tianmu.org/good-work-library/hindu/general-texts/the-upanishads-part-ii-max-muller). Entire bounded dialogue read, not a search snippet. An alternate mirror failed to fetch; it is not the checked witness. This is one named translated witness, not a critical-edition collation.

## Claims and scenes

- G1 — 3.8.1–2: Vācaknavī addresses brāhmaṇas, announces two questions, says if answered none will defeat Yājñavalkya concerning Brahman. He invites questions. She compares them to two pointed arrows of a warrior of Kāśī or Videha. There are no physical weapons in this scene.
- G2 — 3.8.3–4: above heaven, below earth, embracing heaven/earth, past/present/future: in what woven warp-and-woof? Answer: ākāśa (Müller: ether). Spoken “space” is a gloss of the source term, not a claim about modern physics.
- G3 — 3.8.5–7: bows to first answer, announces second; repeats question and answer, then asks what ākāśa is woven in. Preserve this progression rather than inventing a different second question.
- G4 — 3.8.8: Akṣara, imperishable; neither coarse/fine, short/long, red/fluid; without shadow/darkness/air/space/attachment/taste/smell/eyes/ears/speech/mind/light/breath/mouth/measure, neither inside/outside, eats nothing, none eats it. Short selects examples; full retains substantive negations in grouped breath lines. Do not illustrate as a giant object, creator with body, or physical support beam.
- G5 — 3.8.9–11: its command orders sun/moon, heaven/earth, divisions of time, rivers, giving/ritual relations. Ritual and tapas of a thousand years have finite result without knowledge. Departure without knowledge versus knowledgeable brāhmaṇa; unseen seeing, unheard hearing, unperceived perceiving, unknown knowing, no other seer/hearer/perceiver/knower; space woven in Akṣara. Do not turn finite result into “good deeds are worthless,” or interpret brāhmaṇa as a change of birth category. Full includes teachings about knowledge and death in calm religious framing.
- G6 — 3.8.12: Gārgī addresses assembly, praises his answers, says none will defeat him in argument concerning Brahman, then holds peace. No applause, teacher award, humiliation, defeat of Yājñavalkya or claim that this resolves all later debate.

## Boundaries and care

Recension classification pending comparative review. Precise named-witness boundary note: “This telling follows Gārgī's later two-question exchange in Bṛhadāraṇyaka 3.8. The earlier exchange in 3.6 includes Yājñavalkya's harsh warning that her head could fall. We do not combine the two or pretend that warning is absent from the wider text.” Earlier passage was read for this parent-only context. Do not hide it with a claim of uniformly welcoming debate. No modern cosmological equivalence, first-woman claim, first-scientist claim or imagined childhood.

Age 9+, ungated proposal; ideas require thought but syntax should not. Care: martial metaphor only, a brief calm teaching about death in full. Asking questions is welcome in family conversation; source authority does not require a child to agree. Art: adult woman scholar addressing adult sage among adult scholars, attentive and dignified, no physical arrows/bow, no halos or goddess costume, no loom or cosmic substance. Dress/architecture are imaginative illustration, not evidence.
