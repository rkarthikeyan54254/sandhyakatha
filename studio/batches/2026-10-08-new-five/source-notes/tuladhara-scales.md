## Claims and scenes

- T1 — CCLXI: Jājali practises severe asceticism in forest/coast; rain mats hair; stands motionless. Kuliṅga birds build nest in his locks with grass. He allows it out of compassion. Do not invent tree-shaped hair, eggs he puts there, or children's physical imitation.
- T2 — CCLXI: eggs hatch, chicks grow, evening outings, independent outings, whole-day outings, absence five days/return sixth, final absence a month. He waits motionless and finally moves. Pride follows successful care. Preserve sincere compassion before pride; no narrator mocking ascetic.
- T3 — CCLXI: after ritual actions he proclaims merit, unseen voice says he is not equal to Tulādhāra at Vārāṇasī and that even Tulādhāra should not boast thus. Jājali angry, travels in silence, reaches trader. Earlier framing also includes underwater pride/Piśācas; omit that separate opening summary, follow the detailed nest sequence, not two invented consecutive journeys.
- T4 — CCLXI end: Tulādhāra stands, welcomes visitor, knows his penance/nest/pride/voice/anger and asks what he can do. No invented customer, price dispute, shopkeeper lecturing a child or claiming magical scales.
- T5 — CCLXII opening: Jājali asks how trader gained firm knowledge while selling scents, juices, plants. Trader speaks of friendship/good to all; zero harm or least necessary harm; buys and sells without cheating, excludes wine; scales are even toward creatures; no praise/blame, equal regard; harmlessness/fearlessness, thinking/speaking/acting without injury.
- T6 — CCLXII later: trader condemns coercion, enslaving people, harming/load-binding animals, sees plough harm to soil creatures; asks reasoned duty rather than blind inherited custom. Do not collapse into “trade is always good” or “farmers are bad.” This edition's scene is the beginning of a longer debate, NOT a claim Jājali's whole transformation finished here. Later ritual debate and birds' testimony are outside our boundary and must not be invented into the landing.

## Tradition and children

Recension classification pending comparative review. Named Ganguli witness; no all-recension agreement claim. Tradition note: “This is the opening of Jājali and Tulādhāra's discussion in the Mahābhārata's Śānti Parva, sections 261–262 in Ganguli. Their longer discussion continues beyond this telling.” Distinct from Dharma-vyādha: not teaching inherited meat trade/parent duty; here universal friendship, impartial scales and minimizing harm challenge spiritual pride. The juxtaposition is fruitful; do not pretend both debates say identical things.

Age 10+, ungated proposal. Care: prolonged bodily austerity and abstaining from food in source; birds protected; criticism of animal harm and slavery. Extreme practices are not instructions for children. Parent-only question preserves criticism of farming and minimum necessary harm; no tidy absolute rule that survival may harm nothing. No invented surrender or vow at end. Open question asks child about feeling safe with another person.

Art: T4/T5 shop dialogue, adult trader at ordinary plant-goods shop with balance, adult ascetic guest; no birds perched on head in this later moment, no meat shop conflation, no glowing weighing pans, no fabricated customer. Incidental trade vessels and architecture are reconstruction.
