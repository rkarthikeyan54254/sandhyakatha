## Claims and scenes

- U1 — 4.10.1–2: Upakosala Kāmalāyana serves Satyakāma Jābāla, tends fires twelve years. Other pupils allowed home; he is not. Teacher's wife asks husband to teach this diligent student, warns fires may blame him. Teacher travels without teaching. Reason is NOT stated in base text.
- U2 — 4.10.3: student distressed, does not eat; wife urges eating. He speaks of many desires and sorrows. Do not portray fasting as happy shortcut to revelation.
- U3 — 4.10.4–5: fires decide to teach devoted student. Prāṇa/Brahman, ka/Brahman, kha/Brahman. Student understands first, asks about ka/kha. Fires identify ka with kha and conversely. Müller glosses pleasure/ether, Jha discusses transient pleasure versus spiritual bliss. Spoken gloss “joy/space” is not exhaustive doctrine.
- U4 — 4.11–13: Gārhapatya: earth, fire, food, sun; person in sun identified with fire. Anvāhāryapacana: water, directions, stars, moon; person in moon. Āhavanīya: breath, space, heaven, lightning; person in lightning. Each promises protection in this and other world to knowing meditator. Do not assign person in eye to third fire; that is teacher's final teaching.
- U5 — 4.14: fires say teacher will teach way. Teacher returns, notes shining face, asks who taught. Student points at fires; evasive/elliptical reply varies in translations. Use pointing/report without inventing an outright lie. Teacher offers further teaching with lotus-water analogy; student asks to receive it.
- U6 — 4.15: person seen in eye is Self, immortal/fearless/Brahman; names connected with blessings and shining, divine path at death from light/day/bright fortnight/northern six months/year/sun/moon/lightning; nonhuman guide to Brahman, no return. No graduation ceremony or homecoming invented. Short stops at final teaching; full includes path succinctly. Source's outcome is instruction, not an explicitly narrated departure.

## Boundaries

Recension classification pending comparative review. Named-witness boundary note: “The Chāndogya Upaniṣad tells this in 4.10–15. We follow its sequence of the fires' instruction and the teacher's completion. It does not say why the teacher first left without teaching.” Different commentaries supply explanations; none becomes a sourced motive. Compare the previous Satyakāma story for continuity, not import its cattle/bull/birds into this episode. Ka/kha gloss retained carefully; do not claim fire, joy and physical empty air exhaust Brahman.

Age 9+, ungated proposal. Parent care: prolonged waiting, distress and refusing food. Encourage adult support and eating; no initiation ritual reproduced. Teacher's wife is a speaking advocate, not invented named mother. Final parent answers keep religious claims in the source's voice without adjudicating whether fires can speak. Art: pupil as young adult after twelve years of service, seated at respectful distance from three separate simple sacrificial fires, no flame faces, no ka/kha letters, no teacher in instruction moment, no starvation depiction. Altar shapes and ritual layout are illustrative, not a certified śrauta reconstruction.
