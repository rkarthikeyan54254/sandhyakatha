# Art and social proof

Five selected 1448 × 1086 PNG masters; matching WebP hero candidates; fifteen 1200 × 630 PNG/JPG share-card candidates. Exact ImageGen prompts/revision instructions are in `art/prompts.json`. Selected masters were visually inspected individually. Final cards were inspected together in `og-proof-sheet.png`; Hindi and Tamil cards were also examined at full size while repairing spacing.

| Hero | Source moment | Visual fidelity |
|---|---|---|
| Ṛbhu–Nidāgha | Second visit, before the demonstration | Two adults, wood/grass, elephant/ruler; no child climbing teacher |
| Upakosala | Fires' instruction | Young adult after long service, three distinct modest hearths, safe distance; no flame faces or invented deity |
| Mudgala | Heavenly invitation before decision | Ascetic outside car, bells and swans/cranes, unnamed simply dressed messenger; no boarding |
| Tulādhāra | Shop welcome/discussion | Adults treated respectfully; even mechanical scales; plant goods, no meat-shop conflation, no magical scales |
| Gārgī | Later two-question assembly | Adult woman scholar directs inquiry, adult sage/listeners; no arrows/bow, goddess attributes or physical Brahman |

Mudgala V1 was rejected because the messenger looked like a crowned deity; V2 removes crown/peacock/blue sash. Rejected art is retained only as review evidence. Every selected illustration's costume, vessels, architecture and light are artistic reconstruction. Upakosala's altars are not a certified ritual layout; no generated image is historical source evidence.

Share cards use the same uncropped illustration and native title/source-work names. The SandhyaKatha brand remains Latin. An initial Indic rendering bug removed spaces; explicit measured word positions fixed it. Final proof shows distinct words, intact glyphs and no clipped titles. Hindi/Tamil source footer uses its native script and numeric locus. Candidate cards carry ordinary reading copy; approval status lives in the review page, not a false publication claim in the artwork.

Human art approval remains pending. Voice/audio tracks and campaign posting were not requested or represented as completed. Images are saved as candidates and ready to reuse after visual approval.
