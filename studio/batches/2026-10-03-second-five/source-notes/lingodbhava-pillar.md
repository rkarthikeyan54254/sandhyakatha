# Liṅgodbhava source evidence — English draft

Primary witness: *Śiva Purāṇa*, Vidyeśvara Saṃhitā 6–8, J. L. Shastri English translation (with Sanskrit linked on the source pages): [chapter 6](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/shiva-purana-english/d/doc225551.html), [chapter 7](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/shiva-purana-english/d/doc225552.html), [chapter 8](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/shiva-purana-english/d/doc225553.html).

- 6.1–22: Brahmā and Viṣṇu dispute precedence, fight, and launch world-threatening weapons.
- 7.8–14: Śiva appears as a column of fire and receives both weapons.
- 7.15–18: Viṣṇu seeks its root as a boar, Brahmā its top as a swan; Viṣṇu fails.
- 7.19–28: Ketakī falls from the column's middle; Brahmā solicits its false testimony; Viṣṇu confesses he could not find the bottom.
- 7.29–33: Śiva emerges and praises Viṣṇu's truthfulness.
- 8.1–8: Bhairava threatens Brahmā's fifth head; Viṣṇu asks mercy; Śiva stops Bhairava. This passage **does not depict the head cut off**.
- 8.9–21: Brahmā loses separate temples/festivals yet remains essential to sacrifices; Ketakī is excluded as an offering but allowed above Śiva's image and worn by followers.

Editorial checks: No invented visual of a found top; no severing imported from another telling. A distinct [Skanda Purāṇa, Māheśvara Khaṇḍa 1.126.28–30](https://eternalraga.com/en/scriptures/puranas/skanda-purana/1/126) witness says the Ketakī petal had rested on Śiva’s crown, whereas the Vidyeśvara Saṃhitā says the flower fell from the pillar’s middle. This material divergence makes the draft `stability: variant`; the existing canon row currently says `stable` and needs reconciliation before publication. The threatened sword is disclosed in the care note. Human approval remains pending.
