# Rāvaṇa lifts Kailāsa — English source ledger (2026-10-03)

Primary telling for this draft: [*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa*, Uttara Kāṇḍa 16](https://ramayanpath.com/uttar-kand/sarga/16), Sanskrit verses with English translation (site states IIT Kanpur textual source). Uttara Kāṇḍa is a **later compositional layer** in the Rāmāyaṇa tradition, yet it is traditionally transmitted as an integral part of the epic; see the [Princeton critical translation's discussion](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781400884568_A28767195/preview-9781400884568_A28767195.pdf). Parent-facing provenance should state both facts. The online chapter has a verse-count discrepancy in its UI (30 shown, Sanskrit recension elsewhere has ~50), so source locations below are episode sequence plus verse numbers where corroborated, subject to final Sanskrit review.

| Claim | Uttara 16 location / boundary |
|---|---|
| After taking Puṣpaka from Kubera, Rāvaṇa finds its flight stopped near Kailāsa. | opening of 16; preceding episode gives Kubera context |
| Śiva's attendant Nandī says the mountain is closed while Śiva is there. Rāvaṇa mocks Nandī's appearance; Nandī foretells a monkey-led blow to his pride. | early 16, before the lifting |
| Rāvaṇa grasps and shakes Kailāsa; attendants are frightened and Pārvatī clings to Śiva. | middle 16 |
| Śiva presses the mountain with a toe, trapping Rāvaṇa's arms; Rāvaṇa cries out. | middle 16 |
| At his ministers' urging Rāvaṇa recites hymns and sacred texts over a long span; Śiva releases him, gives him the name Rāvaṇa, and later a sword. | latter 16 |

**Do not import:** The chapter does not name the hymns as the surviving *Śiva Tāṇḍava Stotram*, and does not say Rāvaṇa fashioned a vīṇā from his nerves. The draft omits both. The chapter gives a thousand-year span; we need not ask a child to map that number to ordinary time, but must not turn it into a brief apology. Nandī's prophecy points to the later monkey army, without spelling out every subsequent scene in this stand-alone telling.

**Variant:** [*Śiva Purāṇa*, Koṭirudra Saṃhitā 28.60–73](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/shiva-purana-english/d/doc226529.html) tells Rāvaṇa's lifting after Nārada challenges the strength of Śiva's boon. It does not narrate the toe-pinning/name episode in that passage. Do not blend that motivation into the Uttara Kāṇḍa scene. The canon row's present `work` lists both texts; this draft selects the Uttara Kāṇḍa telling and should specify that choice in `source`/`traditionNote`.

**Editorial caveat:** Story follows a figure already known from the Rāmāyaṇa; do not make his harm admirable or imply his later choices were repaired by this meeting. Śiva can recognize fierce devotion and courage without the narrator excusing Rāvaṇa's pride. Human source-fidelity/editorial review remains pending.
