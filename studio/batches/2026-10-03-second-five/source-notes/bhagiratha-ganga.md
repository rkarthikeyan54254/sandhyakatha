# Bhagīratha and Gaṅgā source evidence — English draft

Primary witness: *Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa*, Bāla Kāṇḍa 1.40–44, Sanskrit text and Desiraju Hanumanta Rao verse-by-verse/prose translation: [Sarga 40](https://www.valmikiramayan.net/utf8/baala/sarga40/bala_5F40_prose.htm), [Sarga 42](https://www.valmikiramayan.net/utf8/baala/sarga42/bala_42_prose.htm), [Sarga 43](https://sanskritdocuments.org/sites/valmikiramayan/baala/sarga43/bala_43_prose.htm), [Sarga 44](https://www.valmikiramayan.net/utf8/baala/sarga44/bala_44_prose.htm).

- 1.40–41: Sagara's sons become ashes; Gaṅgā's water is needed for their release.
- 1.42.1–11; 1.44.9–12: Aṃśumān and Dilīpa wished to bring Gaṅgā down but could not.
- 1.42.10–24: Bhagīratha delegates the kingdom, practises tapas at Gokarṇa, asks Brahmā for his ancestors and his family line; Brahmā says Śiva alone can bear Gaṅgā's fall.
- 1.43.1–12: Bhagīratha petitions Śiva; Gaṅgā falls into his jaṭā, remains trapped, then is released after further tapas.
- 1.43.12–32: Seven streams, one following Bhagīratha's chariot; aquatic creatures and onlookers.
- 1.43.33–41: Jahnu drinks and releases Gaṅgā; she reaches the ashes below the ocean and Sagara's sons reach heaven.
- 1.44.1–18: Brahmā gives the name Bhāgīrathī; Bhagīratha makes ancestral water offerings and returns to his city.
