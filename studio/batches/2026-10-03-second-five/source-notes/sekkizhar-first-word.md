# Sēkkiḻār first word — source boundary (2026-10-03)

**Decision:** do not draft or promote `sekkizhar-first-word` against the current canon hook. The received Tamil Śaiva tradition supports Śiva giving Sēkkiḻār the opening word **உலகெலாம் / Ulakelam** at Chidambaram. It does **not** support the hook's extra chronology that he sat unable to find a word **for days**. Treat that as an unsupported embellishment, not a harmless dramatic detail.

The opening verse of the *Periya Purāṇam* itself begins with the word. The divine-word episode is a **later account about the making of** the work, not an episode narrated by the *Periya Purāṇam* itself. The [Tamil Virtual Academy lesson](https://www.tamilvu.org/ta/courses-degree-a011-a0112-html-a01121p4-5335), [Shaivam's introduction](https://shaivam.org/to-practise/life-of-the-sixty-three-nayanmar-periyapuraanam/), and the [Tamil *Sēkkiḻār Svāmikaḷ Purāṇam* transcription](https://www.valaitamil.com/sekkilar-swamigal-puranam_7948.html) all attest the first-word tradition. The [Tamil scholarly examination by Mā. Rāsamāṇikkaṉār](https://www.shaivam.org/devotees/sekkizhar-araichi-nool-rasamanikkanar/) recounts his prayer and the word heard, while explicitly questioning historical claims and the traditional attribution of the later Sēkkiḻār-life text. None gives a multi-day inability to start. The [Tamil Nadu Raj Bhavan press release](https://tnrajbhavantour.tn.gov.in/tnrb-website-archive/Archives/Website1621/PressReleases/2018/PR200518.pdf) says he sat not knowing how to begin, but specifies no days.

The king's request is supported in the later tradition, not in the opening verse itself. Source notes for any future edition must identify that layer precisely and not present a later hagiography as Sēkkiḻār's own testimony. A source-faithful retelling could show the request, the prayer at Chidambaram, and the first word heard without adding a duration or invented failed attempts. That would require revising the canon hook and a full source review; this batch should use another fifth story.

**Proposed substitute:** `ravana-lifts-kailasa` from existing draft canon, anchored in *Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa*, Uttara Kāṇḍa 16. The episode's core actions and sequence are in the [Sanskrit and English chapter text](https://ramayanpath.com/uttar-kand/sarga/16). Be exact about recension: [Śiva Purāṇa, Koṭirudra Saṃhitā 28](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/shiva-purana-english/d/doc226529.html) has a different Kailāsa-lifting context and omits the toe-pinning scene, so it cannot be used as a witness to those details. Do not import the popular claim that Rāvaṇa sang the surviving Śiva Tāṇḍava Stotram; Uttara 16 says only that he recited hymns and sacred texts.
