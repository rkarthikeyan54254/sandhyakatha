# Sukanyā and Cyavana — source note

Primary telling selected: Kisari Mohan Ganguli, *Mahābhārata*, Vana Parva, Tīrtha-yātrā Parva, sections CXXII–CXXIII: https://sacred-texts.com/hin/m03/m03122.htm and https://sacred-texts.com/hin/m03/m03123.htm (checked 2026-10-03).

Parallel: *Bhāgavata Purāṇa* 9.3.2–26, https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/9/3/ (checked 2026-10-03). This differs materially at the three-men choice: in Ganguli's Mahābhārata translation Sukanyā identifies Cyavana herself; the Bhāgavata says she cannot distinguish him and the Aśvins show him to her. The draft follows the Mahābhārata and marks `stability: variant`. It does not combine those endings.

The Mahābhārata witness describes an anthill grown over the ascetic, two thorns piercing his eyes, afflicted soldiers, Sukanyā's confession, and Cyavana demanding her as bride as the condition for forgiving the king. That last event is coercive by modern lights; do not turn it into her free choice. The later choice among three identical young men is a different event. This deserves `gated: true` and a direct parental care note. The Bhāgavata adds blood and a later rebuke from her father, neither imported into this draft. The story's source/age/sensitivity metadata should be aligned in the canon row before publication; current canon row says Bhagavata primary, stable, minAge 9, and no sensitivities.
